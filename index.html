Our Journey & History

Founded in Kasoa, GateWay School Complex began with a vision to provide children in the Central Region with world-class education rooted in discipline.

From a small nursery of ten students, we have evolved into a premier basic educational complex, producing leaders who excel both academically and morally.

"True education is the architecture of the soul and the engine of a nation's progress."
