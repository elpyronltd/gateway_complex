Our Journey & History

Founded in Kasoa, GateWay School Complex began with a simple vision: to provide children in the Central Region with an education that rivals global standards while remaining rooted in Ghanaian values.

What started as a small nursery has grown into a premier basic school (Basic 1-9) known for discipline and academic excellence. Over the years, we have produced students who lead in various sectors across the country.

Our commitment to continuous improvement led to the establishment of the modern science labs and ICT centers that define our campus today.
