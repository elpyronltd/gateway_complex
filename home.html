Our Vision for Basic Education

At GateWay Basic School, located in Kasoa, we are dedicated to providing a strong foundation for learners from Basic 1 to 9. We aim to foster a holistic learning environment that encourages academic excellence, personal growth, and community engagement from an early age.

Our comprehensive basic school program focuses on foundational knowledge, critical thinking, and character development for young learners in Basic 1-9.
